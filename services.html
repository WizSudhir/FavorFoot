How We Approach Care

From Your Condition to the Care Your Condition Requires.

Foot, ankle, leg and wound problems do not always have a single cause or a one-size-fits-all solution. At Favor, we bring clinical evaluation, advanced diagnostics and specialized treatment options together to understand what is affecting you and build a care plan around your condition, healing needs and mobility.

01

Understand

We review your symptoms, history, concerns and goals to understand how your condition affects comfort, function and mobility.

02

Identify the Cause

We combine clinical evaluation with diagnostic information to understand structure, function, circulation and contributing factors.

03

Match Treatment to the Condition

We match treatment to your condition, from wound and limb-preservation care to regenerative medicine, laser therapies, surgery, orthotics and bracing.

04

Protect Healing & Mobility

We track your progress and adjust care to support healing, mobility and prevention of future problems.
