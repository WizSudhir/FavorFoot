Specialized Services

Specialized care for every part of your journey.

From wound and limb-preservation care to advanced diagnostics, surgery and biomechanical support, discover the specialized services available at Favor.

01

Diabetic Limb Salvage & Wound Care

Specialized care for diabetic and complex lower-extremity wounds with a focus on healing, tissue protection and mobility.

02

Regenerative Medicine

Advanced treatment approaches designed to support tissue recovery and address selected musculoskeletal conditions.

03

High-Tech Diagnostics

Modern diagnostic tools help our clinical team better understand structure, function and underlying causes.

04

Laser & Specialized Therapies

Non-invasive and specialized treatment technologies may be incorporated into an individualized care plan when appropriate.

05

Reconstructive Foot Surgery

Custom Orthotics & Bracing
