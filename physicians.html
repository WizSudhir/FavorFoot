A focused approach to lower-extremity care

Experienced care for feet, ankles and complex wounds.

Favor Foot & Ankle Leg / Wound Center is led by Dr. Steven Wells, DPM, PhD. His practice combines general podiatric care with specialized experience in wound care, limb salvage, diabetic foot reconstruction and foot and ankle surgery.

Dr. Wells began his postsecondary education at Lehman College in New York City and earned his Doctorate in Podiatric Medicine from New York College of Podiatric Medicine in 1997. His postgraduate training included an internship at St. Clare Hospital, residency training at Interfaith Medical Center, and a fellowship at SUNY Downstate.

Over the course of his career, Dr. Wells has developed a broad clinical practice spanning podiatric conditions, diabetic foot care, wound management, reconstructive care and surgical and nonsurgical treatment options.

His approach emphasizes understanding the individual patient, evaluating the underlying problem, and selecting an appropriate treatment strategy—from conservative measures when appropriate to advanced interventions for more complex conditions.

Wound Care

Diabetic Foot Care

Comprehensive attention to diabetic foot conditions, neuropathy, ulcers and the factors that can place a limb at risk.

Limb Salvage

A specialized focus on preserving lower-extremity function when complex wounds and diabetic complications threaten a limb.

Reconstructive Care
